Questions

Everything you're probably wondering.

How is the emissions data actually calculated? ⌄

We apply DEFRA's 2023 conversion factors to the material, transport, and processing data you provide at each supply chain stage. Every figure traces back to a cited source, so nothing in the label is an unexplained estimate.

Do I need a sustainability team to set this up? ⌄

No. Most teams paste in existing supplier notes — emails, PDFs, spreadsheet exports — and GreenProof structures them automatically. Typical setup for a first product takes about two minutes.

What do consumers actually see when they scan the label? ⌄

An animated timeline of the product's supply chain, stage by stage, alongside the calculated emissions figure and its source. It's the same underlying data your auditors would review — nothing simplified or hidden.

Can this replace our existing certification process? ⌄

GreenProof isn't a certifying body — it's the traceability layer that makes your existing certifications easier to support with evidence. Many teams use it to prepare cleanly for audits rather than as a replacement for them.

What happens when a supplier or stage changes? ⌄

Update the affected stage and the emissions figure, label, and audit trail recalculate automatically. There's no rebuild or re-submission process — the model is designed to move at the same pace your supply chain does.
