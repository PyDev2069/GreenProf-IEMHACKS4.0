# 🌱 GreenProf — Sustainable Supply Chain Traceability

**GreenProof** is a sustainability platform designed to make product supply chains more **transparent, traceable, and eco-conscious**. 🌍  
🔗 Track every stage — from **raw materials to processing, manufacturing, and shipping**.  
📊 Record key details such as **origin, transport mode, distance, and courier information**.  
🤖 Generate AI-powered **Carbon Cards** using a locally running **Ollama LLM**, including a sustainability rating and CO₂e estimate.  
📱 Share verified product footprints through **public token-based links and QR codes**.  
📈 Monitor products through a **searchable dashboard, progress indicators, and notifications**.  
🛠️ Built with **Flask, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login, Jinja2, Vanilla JS**, and a custom **forest-green & gold UI** with light/dark mode.

- ## 🚀 Business Promotion

### 🌱 Make Sustainability Visible. Make Trust Verifiable.

**GreenProof** helps small businesses, product sellers, and emerging brands communicate the environmental impact of their products with greater transparency.

From sourcing raw materials to manufacturing and delivery, GreenProof enables businesses to document their supply chain, estimate their carbon footprint, and turn complex sustainability data into simple, shareable **Carbon Cards**.

With public verification links and QR codes, businesses can give customers an easy way to explore a product's sustainability journey — helping build **trust, transparency, and brand value**.

> **Our vision:** Make sustainable and transparent supply chains accessible to businesses of every size.

### 📈 Business Potential

GreenProof can help businesses:

- 🌍 Demonstrate their commitment to sustainability
- 📊 Understand and communicate their product's environmental footprint
- 🤝 Build customer trust through transparent supply-chain information
- 📱 Share sustainability information through QR codes and public product pages
- 🌱 Differentiate their products in an increasingly sustainability-conscious market
